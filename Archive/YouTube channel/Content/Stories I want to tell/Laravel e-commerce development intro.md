Hi, I am Amir and in this short video, I am gonna tell you about the e-commerce development course I want to start over on YouTube.

I’ve been a web developer for years now and I developed production-level e-commerce systems for different types of needs from jewelry shops to systems for selling online courses or even an online grocery and food shop.

Every e-commerce platform has some key elements, like:

- Products →tags, images
- Comments
- Users
- Invoices

In my course, you will learn about these key elements and how to implement these features with best practices in Laravel. Besides this, we will talk about authorization and security concerns surrounding e-commerce in a business environment.

So after this course which will be published over YouTube during the next weeks and months, you will be able to do real-world Laravel projects for businesses or finally start that side project of yourselves that you have in your mind for a while, which for sure I’ll be happy to hear about 🙂

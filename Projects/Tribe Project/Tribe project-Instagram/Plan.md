# Results from the research

- Only 10.9 percent of Instagram accounts ever get to the magic 10k number!
- It seems that it takes 6 to 9 months to reach 10K followers on Instagram

# Goal

The goal is to create a network over Instagram with over 10K followers.

- We want over 10K followers
- We want a good engagement(like/comment/etc.)
- We want a good conversation rate(close to 2%)

# Finance

Let’s say we want to put 200$ per month into this project. Assuming it takes 6 months to reach 10K followers(a bit optimistic prediction) we’ll spend 6*200$=1200$.

**Cost of project: 6 months * 200$ per month = 1200$**

Assumptions:

- We sell products for an average of 20$
- The conversion rate could be from 1% to 2%

| Product price | Conversion rate | Converted numbers | Monthly Revenue |

| 20$ | 0.8% | 80 | 1600$ |
| 20$ | 1.2% | 120 | 2400$ |
| 20$ | 2% | 200 | 4000$ |
| 50$ | 0.8% | 80 | 4000$ |
| 50$ | 1.2% | 120 | 6000$ |
| 50$ | 2% | 200 | 10000$ |

# Workforce

One person is needed for account management, content creation, and planning. 
The role should include:

- Initial research phase
- Content creation
- Engaging with the community

We could start with **1$ per hour of work**, assuming it’s less than 200 hours of work per month anyway. There will be a buffer for extra costs and bonuses.

To motivate the employee we can double the rate when we reach the **10K goal**, **2$ per hour **which for a full-time job creates a cost of 2$ * 160 = 320$.

During these 6 months, we need to prepare and create products that we can sell using this channel. The revenue of this project depends on this.

# Content

We need a research phase to know better what content should be published.
It’s suggested to have **at least one week buffer of content.**

# My Notes

## Why do people feel lost?

- You don't know where to go
- You don't know where you are
- You don't know how to go from A to B

Nobody really knows what they're doing with their life, you don't need to map your entire life. It's a moving thing.

Practice of "Wayfinding" from the book "Designing Your Life": Process of figuring out where to go even when you don't have a clear destination in mind.

## 5 Ways to find where we trying to go

This video will cover 5 different ways to sort out the question “where are we trying to go”

- Brainstorm what service you'd offer
- Reduce the effect of fear
- Envision your dream life
- Getting at the issue using other people
- Begin with the end in mind

### The service you offer

What service you would offer?

> [!note]+ ### **TED talk prompt**
> Imagine a TED talk where you are the speaker but in 20 years from now, what you are talking about, what's so inspiring about it?
> 
> Why we're talking about service?
> 
> The recipe for a meaningful life is not about getting famous or more money, it's the sense of service. It should not be life-changing, it would be just as inspiring if you talk about how to be a great parent or a teacher.

> [!note]+ ### **The talent service prompt**
> If you have all the money and all the time in the world, How would you use your talents and skills to serve others?

> [!note]+ ### **The eulogy prompt**
> Imagine at your funeral, what would people positively impacted by your work say?

**Write down** your answers, don't solve this in your mind. It would be recommended to write it down in a handbook

### Reduce the effect of fear

Emotional fear stops us of from doing what we relly want. Most of time we comply with family or social rules.

> [!note]+ ### The fearless dream prompt
> What would you do, if you knew you could not fail at it?
> 
> - One way to think about this is to categorize as health, work, relationship aspects.
> - The other way is to think about what you like to do, learn, try, or experiment with?
> - What impact do you like to have?

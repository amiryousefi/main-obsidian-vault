### Why should I write online?

**No gatekeepers**: No big publishers needed. write and publish on social media in front of your audience.

**Feedback**: You get feedback quickly from your audience and followers. Writing online has a very short feedback loop.

### How to Write online effectively

- **Forest of ideas**: Generate lots of ideas and publish consistently.
We have yet to determine which idea resonates with the audience. Do not start writing a journey in a blog. write on platforms that network effects like social media, Medium, etc.
- **Feedback Cave**: Facing feedback online is hard. Feedback is gonna be immediate. There is nowhere to hide.
> There are 2 types of writers today: those who use data to inform and improve their writing, and those who fail.

Quantitative, qualitative, performance, and comments metrics data will help. 
- **Pool of analysis**: analyze and spot patterns from the data. Big authors now started with their writings online and just turned their best article online into a book. 

### How to make money writing online.

- Attention modal: Clicks, ads, affiliates, sponsorships.
- Paywall modal: Charge for some of your writings.
> To make a living as a craftsperson, photographer, musician, designer, author, animator, app maker, entrprenur, or inventor your need only one thousands of true fans. — Kevin Kelly
- Creating a course
- Services modal: ghostwriting, copywriting, etc.

*“I’d rather have 1,000 loyal customers than 100,000 unsupportive fans”*

[Desiree Peralta](https://medium.com/@dessyperalt) ran a service based exclusive content on her YouTube channel to produce content for $5.

Here’s her suggestions.

Currently, there are platforms for any format in which you want to implement your subscription:

- You can use [Substack](https://substack.com/) for monetizing words. This is one of the largest platforms that offers a subscription community for writers who want to monetize their newsletter.
- You can use YouTube and [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/en/creators) to monetize videos and pictures. YouTube already has an integrated subscription model, and you can offer exclusive videos to your audience. Patreon allows you to monetize any digital content you want.
- And you can use [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) to monetize everything else. This platform has tools to charge clients a fee for any type of service (or even products) you want to offer.

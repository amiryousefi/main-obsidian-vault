### **Attract the Right Eyeballs**

Content is the pillar of any passive income machine and it’s often overlooked. A passive income machine needs email addresses:
Create content. Collect email addresses. Simple.

### **Spend 1-Hour per Day for a Year Building an Online Community**

You need a  place for humans to congregate. Email, Telegram or Discord group, whatever works for you.

> There’s zero point in having a product or service without a community.

Many people do this back to front. You need a community to figure out what the hell you are doing. What are the problems people have? A comunity is where you go to listen. It’s where you find the ideas to generate the passive income in first place.

### **The Biggest Problem with Online Communities**

*Rule: Never ask for anything in the first 12 months.
*Most of you can’t do this. The temptation is too great to make money online.
*Hard lesson: people don’t want to be sold to.*

The trick is to focus on the needs of your community. Solve their problems for free for a year.

### **Do This in Year 2 to Build an Unstoppable Passive Income Machine**

Ask for only on thing. The best way is to pretend you get one ask with your community.

**Mindset:** not everybody in your community is a customer. 90% of them will never buy anything from you. 10% of people will though. That will feed your passive income machine. The other 90% still have enormous value because they will share your work and increase the reach.

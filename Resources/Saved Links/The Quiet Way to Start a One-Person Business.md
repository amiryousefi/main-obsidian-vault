Main takeaways:

- **Tell no one your plan**
Don’t let your ego force you to post it on social media.
- **Activate monk mode**
Focus for say 6-12 months and then you’ll see what’s possible
- **Disappear for 12 months
**To intensely focus on your business, minimize socialization, and filter the friends that really understand you.
- **Use the dark hours**
Right after your 9-5 work hours start working on your own business. Or on the weekends. After 10 P.M and before 9 A.M distractions are few and silence is magnificent.
- **Dark mode on all apps**
No, we don’t mean dark background and white text. Of course, you can do so if you like it.
Turn off all notifications, and unsubscribe from almost every newsletter. Use social media at strategic times with an exact purpose in mind.
Stop mindlessly scrolling and use social media to post and look for ideas related to your business.

A one-person business isn’t about ego and noise. It’s about freedom.

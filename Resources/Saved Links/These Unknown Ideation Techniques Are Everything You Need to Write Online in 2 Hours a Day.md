# 4 ways to come up with killer ideas:

## Connect the dots

combine multiple ideas to create new great ideas.
Most of them may suck but some will lead to great thoughts.

## Build your idea muscle 

You need to practice coming up with ideas.

You can do this in several ways, like for headlines of an article:


- Rapid fire article headline ideas
- Sprinkle in some numbers
- Add a timestamp
- Specificity

Do this with all ideas on a daily basis and you will strengthen your idea muscle.

## Notice the world around you

That the best way to come up with ideas was to pay close attention to the world around you.

Many great ideas come from noticing a problem in the market. Once you find the problem, you can start to build a solution for it.

Finding the problem is innovation for itself.

## Oil the machine

You can spark ideas by creating the right conditions around you.

— Raymond Inman

- Rest and relax
- Let your mind flow

## What are Deadlines?

Deadline is a specific time and date that we choose before which we have to complete our task. Deadlines should help us plan things out since we know when we have to finish them and (potentially) how long these things should take.

Here's a great explanation of deadlines by one-and-only Seth Godin:

*"Deadlines work. They work because they focus the mind and create urgency. They work to get us to file our taxes or finish an assignment. They’re an external lever for the work we have to do."*

*"On the other hand, dessert works too. You don’t need an external force to encourage you to eat dessert after you’ve finished all your vegetables. It’s something you get to do, not something you have to do."*

*"You can build a work life around deadlines. You can procrastinate, pay the late fines and push through the last minute emergencies because you need all of that in order to get to ‘have to’ mode."*

*"Or, you can follow the path of the most productive and happy people you know. By redefining the work you’ve chosen to do as something you get to do."*

## How I Use It

I'm not really a huge fan of deadlines. Sure, setting an end time for any task is crucial. Essentially, there has to be one. But, I'm not a fan of building my life around deadlines because, similarly to goals, they lead to some unwanted consequences like putting off the task until the last minute or living in the 'have to' mode. 

And, to be honest, I'd much rather work on things that are fun so I don't have to push myself to complete them. In this case, there's no need to think about productivity per se, as once you're having fun, productivity takes care of itself.

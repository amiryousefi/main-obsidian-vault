## What are Goals?

Well... we all know goals. It's a specific thing, end state, or a place that we want to end up with/at. Usually, goals are combined with deadlines so a goal could be to lose 10 lbs by the end of 2021.

## How I Use It

Nowadays when I have a team of around 13 people I'm getting more and more inclined towards using goals as it's the simplest and most efficient way to get people aligned and focused around one idea. Once you have a goal, you know exactly what you're working towards and that makes it easier to plan and take action. Although I make sure to set input goals rather than output goals so it's always within my control.

And if you want some more guidance on how to choose meaningful goals I highly recommend you read How will you measure your life? by Clayton Christensen. It's a great book that will feed you with some ideas on how to prioritise your work and spend more time chasing things that are meaningful to you.

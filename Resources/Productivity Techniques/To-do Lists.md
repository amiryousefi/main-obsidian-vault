## What are To-do Lists?

Making to-do lists is probably one of the most common productivity techniques out there. Probably because it simply works. It's so easy to forget about tasks that it's just necessary to have a way to store them. And to-do lists make for a perfect place to do that.

A To-do list is simply a list of things that we want (or have 😅) to do on a specific day/week/month.

## How I Use It

Nowadays, I use a combination of a digital and physical to-do list. For the prior, I use an app called Roam Research and for hand-written to-do lists, I use Analog Starter Kit from Ugmonk.

But, at the end of the day, it doesn't matter how you store your tasks or if you use one app over the other. The most important thing is to have a place to store these tasks. And, I highly recommend you to use Daily Highlight on your daily and weekly to-do list. This way, you won't run into analysis paralysis which may stop you from crossing off any of your to-dos.

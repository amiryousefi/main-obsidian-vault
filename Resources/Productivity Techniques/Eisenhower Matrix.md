## What's the Eisenhower Matrix?

Eisenhower Matrix is a way to tame an overflowing to-do list. Basically, you need to take all your tasks and split them between four categories:

- Tasks that are urgent and important - these are the tasks that you should do now.
- Tasks that are urgent but not important - should be delegated.
- Tasks that are not urgent but important - should be scheduled.
- Tasks that are not urgent and not important - should be deleted.
![[7407a2c34a7cb94a7bf9e7f07e09a4cb.png]]

After you split your tasks between four categories you should be left with a clear picture of what you have to do now, schedule for later, delegate, and get rid of. No more analysis paralysis.

## How I Use It

I think this productivity technique itself is great and can be very useful but I don't use it personally. At least not actively. I definitely divide my tasks between those four buckets but I don't spend time making an actual matrix, it happens more on the fly as I'm thinking about the task.

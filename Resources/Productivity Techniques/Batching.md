## What's Batching?

Batching is grouping similar tasks so you can do all of them in one go.

## How I Use It

Personally, I use it for filming my YouTube videos. Instead of shooting a few vids across the week, I shoot 3-5 videos at one go. This way I don't have to set up cameras and mics over and over again and once I get into the video-recording mood, shooting 4 videos in a row becomes an easy thing to do.

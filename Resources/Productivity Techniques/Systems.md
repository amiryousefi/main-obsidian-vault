## What are Systems?

Systems are sets of tasks, routines, and activities that bring a specific result. When we want to achieve something, we tend to think goal-wise focusing on a singular outcome. But a much better approach is to always tackle goals with systems - chains of processes that actually lead to that goal. In the first scenario we're blindly walking towards some destination while in the second, we're using an actual map.

## How I Use It

Whenever I struggle to get something done, I try to break it into smaller pieces and look at it in terms of processes rather than a single goal. Once I know what it takes to get there, I follow the system and try to refine it along the way. Knowing the goal (outcome/task you want to get done) is good but it's not enough to achieve it. You need to know the right path to that goal and thinking in terms of systems and finding that path is what always helps me get things done no matter how small or big these tasks are.

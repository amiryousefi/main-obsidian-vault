## What's Daily Highlight?

The daily highlight is a productivity technique from my all-time favourite productivity book called Make Time by John Zeratsky and Jake Knapp.

The idea is to choose one specific task that we want to get done during the day. And while on most days we have to do much more than a single task, setting a daily highlight makes it more probable that we're going to finish - at least - that one particular thing.

An overly long to-do list may lead to analysis paralysis and eventually us not taking action because of the feeling of being overwhelmed. But whenever I set a daily highlight I almost always get that one thing done.

## How I Use It

I choose one task on a specific day that I want to get done. It's usually the most urgent and/or the most important thing that needs to be done on that day or during this week. Setting it as a daily highlight is just another level of motivation, discipline, and prioritisation that helps me to get it done.

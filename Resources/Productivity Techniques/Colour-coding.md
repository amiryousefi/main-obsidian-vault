## What's Colour Coding?

Colour-coding is a simple technique of assigning colours to different types of tasks based on some pre-defined system. 

## How I Use It

Honestly, I haven't found it to be useful (hence, my one-colour calendar) but many people swear by it. For example, my fellow YouTuber and AppSumo CEO, Noah Kagan colour-codes his calendar based on different categories of tasks. He uses different colours for growth activities, leisure time, or gym so eventually, his calendar looks kind of like this...

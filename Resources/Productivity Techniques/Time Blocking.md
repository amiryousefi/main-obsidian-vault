## What's Time Blocking?

Time blocking or Timeboxing is simply setting a fixed amount of time for a given task. This way you end up with blocks of time in your calendar which leaves no room for wondering what to do or for how long you should work on any given task.

## How I Use It

I use it for nearly each and every task in my professional and personal life from blocking out time for my book writing and filming sessions to scheduling takeaways with my friends.

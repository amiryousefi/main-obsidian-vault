## What's the 5-Minute Rule?

The 10-minute rule is fairly similar to its tinier 2-minute sister as it's also about building momentum during times when my inner wasteman takes a lead. 

**Essentially, whenever I find myself procrastinating on a task, I tell myself that I'm going to do it for just 5 minutes. No more, no less. 5 minutes on the timer.**

Obviously, 5 minutes are not going to push the task forward, especially if it's a complex thing that I have to work on. But... 5 minutes is enough to get you started and build momentum. It's a little spark that, in the beginning, doesn't light the whole fire but is eventually (when you think about it) the actual cause of it. 

## How I Use It

I use a 5-minute hourglass that just sits on my desk and whenever I feel sluggish and just don't want to start working on a task, I flip it over and force myself to start working. This little push is usually enough to break the chains of procrastination and build the momentum and get me going.

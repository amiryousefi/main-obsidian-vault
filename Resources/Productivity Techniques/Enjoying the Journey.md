## What's 'Enjoying the Journey'?

Enjoying the journey is really the key to all productivity. As long as you enjoy working towards your goals and enjoy your daily work in general you won't ever have to worry or think about productivity. It'll take care of itself.

## How I Use It

Whenever I find myself struggling to do a task like this one day when I was trying to negotiate myself to write a few pages of my book, my (now-ex) flatmate Sheen asked me if this whole 'writing a book thing' wasn't supposed to be fun. I immediately realised that I forgot about the "enjoy the journey" thing and that I fixated myself on achieving the goal without having fun along the way. As soon as I switched my mindset to "let's have fun and enjoy the process" there was no only "having to write" only "getting to write."

There's also this one tip from Tim Ferris which is to ask ourselves “what would this look like if it were easy?” whenever we struggle to do something. Now, whenever I stumble upon a hard-to-crack task I make sure to approach it as it was actually easy to do and have fun while trying to make it easy and enjoyable.

## What's Delegation?

Delegation is a concept that I first learnt about in Tim Ferriss' 4-Hour Workweek and later from Naval Ravikant who has this idea of assigning a dollar value to your time and then an aspirational hourly rate. Once you do that, you should **delegate anything that's below that hourly rate so you can focus exclusively on high-leverage work**. 

If there's any work you don't enjoy that can be outsourced for less than that your hourly rate (like cleaning your flat lol), then it might be a good idea to do that.

## How I Use It

When I first started on YouTube, my dollar value of time was about $25/hour. So the first part of my business that I actually delegated was video editing. I knew that my time could be better spent on other parts of the business and there's another factor of expertise. There are hundreds of talented video editors out there so why should I reinvent the wheel if I can just smartly invest my money.

And these days I also delegate many parts of my business to others as I know they can do a better job than if I were to do it myself. And I get more time to focus on things that I enjoy and/or I'm good at.

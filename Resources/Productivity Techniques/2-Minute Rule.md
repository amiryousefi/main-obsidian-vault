## What's the 2-Minute Rule?

The 2-minute rule is a very simple razor popularised by David Allen in his book, Getting Things Done and it goes something like: **If a task will take less than 2 minutes, do it right now. If it will take longer, write it down.**

## How I Use It

While very simple this is actually a good starting point when you don't feel like doing anything. Whenever I experience productivity slumps, I think about the simplest task that's on my to-do list and that won't take longer than 2 minutes. This way I can build momentum to hopefully find energy and motivation for more demanding activities.

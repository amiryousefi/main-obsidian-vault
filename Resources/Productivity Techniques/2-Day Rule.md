## What's the 2-Day Rule?

A 2-day rule is an alternative approach to "sticking to your schedule." When we first start building a habit, it's tempting to set a very optimistic 7- or 30-day streak goal. But that's not very realistic and will likely result in a sooner-than-later failure and discouragement.

So what Matt proposes is that we have this one-day window where we can actually skip our goal, habit, or activity. But we can't do that two days in a row. 

**“You won’t allow yourself to skip a goal/habit/activity for two days in a row.”**

This way we can keep our goals and schedule flexible and realistic without falling out of the habit-building loop.

## How I Use It

I tried using a 2-day rule back when I was regularly going to the gym but it didn't really work for me. Maybe because I'm not as on a gym hype as Matt is. 😅 In fact, what worked for me was getting an accountability buddy who'd make sure I train regularly and do skip gym sessions too often.

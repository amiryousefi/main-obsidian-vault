## What's Speed Listening?

Speed listening is simply listening to audiobooks and podcasts on (at least) 2x original speed.

If there's one hill that I'll die on, it's listening to audiobooks and podcasts on double (or triple) speed. This is the one habit that changed my life and something that I strongly believe in. 

The main benefit of speed listening is that you can consume more stuff. I've been able to listen to twice as many audiobooks and podcasts for the last two years which is a tremendous value. Secondly, speed listening reduces the time cost for any type of content. Listening to an 8-hour audiobook is a hard choice to make but if that same book will take only 4 hours, it's an easy sell.

So, not only it helps me consume more stuff in less time it also creates an incentive to continue reading new books and listening to new podcasts.

## How I Use It

Pretty simple. I listen to all of the audiobooks and podcasts on (at least) 2x speed and if I want to get to the meat of an interview or some book, I might even listen to it on a 3x speed. At first, it might seem weird or even unusable but you'll quickly get used to it and after experiencing all the benefits, listening to an audiobook on 1x speed will seem extremely slow. I promise. 😅

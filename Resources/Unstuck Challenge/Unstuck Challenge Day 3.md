But the fact you’re still opening these emails is a great sign, because today I’m talking about one of the most important steps to getting unstuck ―

**Letting go of our past baggage.**

Sometimes, the reason we feel trapped in our current slump isn’t because we don’t have clarity or because we don’t know what to do.

It’s because we’re weighed down by the emotional baggage from our past failed attempts to change our life.

We tend to hold on to our broken habit streaks and unmet goals as “proof” that we’ll never succeed. And the fear of failing (again) can paralyze us from taking meaningful action.

But if we don’t let go of our past failures or our excuses, it becomes harder for us to move forward. We run the risk of making the same mistakes or stumbling over the same mindset traps that tripped us up the first time.

That’s why today is about reframing your excuses and ditching your emotional baggage for good.

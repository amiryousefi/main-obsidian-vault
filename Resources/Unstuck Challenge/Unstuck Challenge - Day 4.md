Welcome to Day 4!

**Today is all about the power of making a decision.**

By now, you’ve identified multiple areas of your life you’re hoping to improve.

And you may be tempted to overhaul every single one of those areas at once so you can create your dream life even faster.

![[32.png]]

But every time I’ve tried to do this, I’ve only ended up further away from achieving my goals because I’d inevitably burn out from all the changes.

Instead, I’ve found it’s far more productive to focus on improving one area of your life at a time.

But how are you supposed to decide which change to work on first when they all feel so important?

That’s exactly what I’m going to help you decide in today’s video.

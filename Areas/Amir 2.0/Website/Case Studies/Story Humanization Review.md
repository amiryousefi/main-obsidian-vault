# Story Humanization Review

Review of the English stories for amiryousefi.com, before they go live. All three edited stories are in one note: [[Case Study Stories (Humanized)]].

Stories covered:
- [[Today's Crypto Story (English)]]

The Oxin story is still a draft and was skipped.

## What changed

- Every em and en dash was replaced with a colon, comma, full stop or plain hyphen. Number ranges now read 2019-2020, and the year headings read "## 2015 - First engagement: ...".
- AI-ish phrasing was cut: dramatic "not X, it was Y" contrasts, neat moral endings, triple adjectives, filler words like genuinely, literally and exactly, and stock phrases like "table stakes", "compounding" and "ossifying".
- Passive sentences now use the "I/we" voice. For example, "Next.js with SSR was chosen for SEO" became "We picked Next.js with SSR for SEO".
- No facts changed. Every number matches the original, heading and paragraph counts are the same, links are untouched, and each story is 0-2% shorter. TodaysCrypto needed the fewest edits.

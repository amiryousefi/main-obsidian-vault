## Overview of the relationship

The chat spans **29 November 2015 to 27 December 2023** — eight years — but the actual working relationship is concentrated in four distinct, non-contiguous stints separated by long silences:

1. **Nov 2015 – Jan 2016** — Onboarding as an outside/contract consultant (Slack + likely WordPress-era work), ending in an unexplained silence and a passive-aggressive account deactivation from Masoud.
2. **May – Sept 2017** — A second stint, restarted warmly, that runs through active recruiting/hiring work and price-list/catalog work, and ends in an explicit, angry falling-out over Amir going unresponsive and not delivering committed work.
3. **Jul 2019 – Oct 2020** — A third, formally negotiated stint (written تفاهم‌نامه/agreement, salary, blocked/held pay, a promissory note as guarantee) covering the Laravel-era rebuild and a UX critique of a product called "عیار". This tapers off around the final paperwork/settlement in October 2020.
4. **Oct 2022 & Dec 2023** — Two isolated, one-off technical-favor exchanges with no ongoing engagement — the relationship's last embers.

## Phase 1 — Onboarding and first contract (29 Nov 2015 – 23 Jan 2016)

The very first message is the literal start of the professional relationship — worth preserving in full as the opening beat of the whole story:

> **29.11.2015 20:32** — Masoud Abadi: سلام،وقت بخیر،دعوتنامه اسلک برای شما ارسال شد.
> **29.11.2015 20:58** — Amir Yousefi: سلام
> جوین شدم به تیم، ممنون
> **29.11.2015 22:26** — Masoud Abadi: ممنون از شما،خوش اومدید

*(Slack invite sent → Amir confirms he's joined the team → welcomed. This is the formal start of Amir's engagement with Sayman Digital.)*

On 2 December 2015 the commercial terms of the first contract are set — a useful anchor for the rate history:

> **02.12.2015 20:22** — Masoud Abadi: سلام،وقت بخیر،خوب هستید؟
> فایلش موجود هست،میدم خدمتتون،مدت صحبت نکردیم،و مبلغم به ازای سه روز در هفته ششصد هست

*(Contract draft requested; rate set at 600 [toman, presumably per day or per stated unit] for three days/week — no fixed contract duration agreed yet.)*

On 25 December 2015 there's a late-night personal exchange — Masoud and Pejman (Pezhman Shakouri, co-founder) checking in with real concern after Amir went quiet for a stretch and reveals he'd had a case of severe food poisoning/hospitalization. Notable mainly for showing genuine warmth early in the relationship — skipped from verbatim quoting here as it's personal/health chat, not scope-relevant, except to note the trust and closeness it reflects.

The phase ends coldly. After weeks of unanswered outreach, Masoud sends this on 23 January 2016 — the clearest single artifact of how the *first* stint ended:

> **23.01.2016 22:06** — Masoud Abadi: ازونجایی که متاسفانه تماس های ما چه با واسطه،چی بی واسطه،چه به کمک تماس تلفنی با دوستان شما،چه از طریق پیام کوتاه،چه از طریق تلگرام در طی هفته های اخیرهیچ کدوم موفقیت آمیز نبوده،متاسفانه حسابهای کاربری شما بصورت موقت غیرفعال میشن تا اینکه بصورت حضوری شما رو در دفتر سایمان دیجیتال زیارت کنیم،به امید اون روز در آینده ی نزدیک

*(Since all attempts to reach Amir — direct, via mutual friends, SMS, Telegram — failed over recent weeks, his user accounts are being temporarily deactivated until an in-person meeting happens.)* No reply from Amir follows. This is the end of stint 1 — a quiet, unresolved lapse in communication rather than an explicit resignation or firing.

## Phase 2 — Second stint: hiring, catalog work, and a hard falling-out (29 May – 5 Sept 2017)

The conversation resumes 16 months later (29 May 2017) with warm, elaborate reopening pleasantries from Masoud, and by 8–9 July 2017 Amir is clearly back working day-to-day: Slack access is reactivated, and Masoud explicitly frames it as a continuation, not a new start —

> **08.07.2017 11:32** — Masoud Abadi: طبق معمول مکاتبات مون توی اسلک پابرجاست

*(As usual, our correspondence stays on Slack — i.e., "business as usual," implying the relationship simply picked back up.)*

Substantive threads in this phase:

- **Recruiting/hiring became one of Amir's responsibilities.** Repeated back-and-forth screening candidate résumés together (e.g., 12–22 July 2017, discussing candidates like "فرزاد بابامیری" and others), posting job ads to Telegram recruiting channels, and jointly interviewing people.
- **Wholesale phone/electronics price-list work.** On 9 July 2017 Masoud sent Amir extensive raw wholesale pricing catalogs (iPhone, Samsung, Xiaomi, LG, HTC, etc.) — confirming Sayman Digital's core business at the time was an online marketplace/price-comparison and reseller platform for mobile phones and electronics, not a pure software project. Amir was doing catalog/pricing-data work alongside development.
- **A small technical exploration**: on 13 July 2017 Masoud asked about building a Telegram bot for a channel giveaway/raffle system, and Amir sketched the mechanism (bot membership check + code-based draw), estimating "a week, give or take" to build — a small but concrete example of Amir being consulted on technical feasibility and scoping.
- Infrastructure/access requests threaded through: GitLab account/group setup ("saymandigital"), Slack re-invites, VPS ticket requests to Pejman, and a shared test ERP (Odoo) instance Amir stood up on 8 August 2017 (`http://151.80.211.153:8069/`).

**The falling-out.** Through August 2017 Masoud's messages show escalating frustration at Amir going quiet and missing commitments (19–23 August: "چرا هیچ خبری از سمت شما نیست", "قراره همینطوری ادامه بدید؟"). It comes to a head on 26 August – 5 September 2017, when Amir explains he can no longer commit to both dev and technical-management work under the current financial/time terms and effectively signals he's stepping back:

> **26.08.2017 09:06** — Amir Yousefi: ...بیشتر از این هم فکر نکنم نیازی به حضور من باشه چون طبق صحبت هامون شرایط مالی یا شرایط زمانی شما قابل انعطاف نیست که فکر میکنم معنیش این باشه جای من از هر نیروی دیگه ای میتونین استفاده کنین قطعا هزینه اش هم براتون کمتره...

Masoud's reply is long, direct, and clearly hurt — accusing Amir of contradicting earlier commitments, disappearing for days without notice, and breaching trust that had been extended more than to other team members:

> **26.08.2017 12:01–12:10** — Masoud Abadi: ...شما تشریف میار دفتر سایمان دیجیتال، فرمایش میکنی، نباید ما رو تنها بزارید، شما تمایل داری توی سایمان دیجیتال حتمن باشی، میرید برنامه ی جدیدتون رو با برنامه ی قدیمی ست میکنید و بما میگید اما بعد از چند روز کاملا صحبت های عکس ارائه میکنی
> این در حالی هستش که من روی شما حساب های خیلی زیادی باز کردم، حسابی که روی هیچ یک از بچه های تیم باز نمیکنم و نکردم اما این کار شما، بی حرمتی هستش...

The last message of this phase (5 September 2017) is Masoud, unanswered, calling out the pattern directly — a hard, explicit end to stint 2:

> **05.09.2017 11:49** — Masoud Abadi: چی باعث میشه شما واقعن اینطور رفتار کنید آقای یوسفی؟ ...
> من فکر میکردم واقعن شما هرجا هم باشی و یا هر جا مشغول کار باشی، ما با هم دوست میمونیم نه اینکه شما تا هستی، دوستی، وقتی میری سعی میکنی ضربه هم بزنید

No reply from Amir follows for nearly two years.

## Phase 3 — Third stint: renegotiation, formal agreement, and the Laravel/عیار era (21 Jul 2019 – Oct 2020)

The conversation resumes 21 July 2019, and unusually, Masoud opens by directly naming the two prior ruptures and asking Amir point-blank whether he's satisfied with how he'd left things — a remarkably candid re-engagement:

> **22.07.2019 11:54** — Masoud Abadi: از اینکه آخرین سری از ما جدا شدید، الان راضی هستید؟ من بدون تعارف سوال میکنم،ببخشید
> **22.07.2019 12:21** — Masoud Abadi: از مثال من دلخور نشید ولی میگن آدم عاقل از یک سوراخ دوبار گزیده نمیشه. من دوبار گزیده شدم اما قرار باشه بار سومی باشه،خیلی متفاوت میشه

*("A wise person isn't bitten twice from the same hole. I've been bitten twice — if there's a third time, it'll be very different." Direct acknowledgement this would be a third attempt, and it would come with harder guarantees.)*

Amir doesn't dodge it:

> **22.07.2019 12:05** — Amir Yousefi: اتفاقی که افتاد درست نبود، منم متاسف و ناراحت هستم و خودم رو هم مقصر می‌دونم.

What follows over the next weeks is a genuinely substantive negotiation, unusually well-documented:

- **13 August 2019** — Amir writes himself a structured self-assessment/negotiating memo (pros/cons, target salary of 4.5M toman/month, wanting "a key role in projects," minimum guaranteed pay vs. task-based bonus split, a blocked/held portion of salary released twice a year, annual income projection of ~54M toman). This is a rare first-person artifact of Amir's own decision-making, not just dialogue.
- **14–15 August 2019** — Masoud drafts and the two jointly edit a formal **تفاهم‌نامه همکاری** (cooperation memorandum) with numbered clauses covering: 3M/month fixed pay + 1.5M/month held ("blocked") pay released up to 70% every 6 months as a quality-control lever; social insurance; discretionary bonuses/Eid bonus separate from base pay; and — reflecting the 2017 breakdown directly — a clause (#9) explicitly citing "two prior instances of unexplained absence" and requiring immediate notice of any disruption, plus (#10) a **100 million toman promissory note (سفته)**, co-signed, held as a guarantee against nonperformance. This formalization is a direct, textual consequence of the 2017 conflict.
- **22 August 2019** — Amir delivers a detailed, professional UX/UI critique of a product referred to as "عیار" (a multi-vendor price-comparison/marketplace front end) — inconsistent color palette, logo/identity inconsistency, information-density problems on the product page, filter/UX issues, typography inconsistency. This is the clearest evidence of Amir's actual scope of work in this stint: front-end/UX review and design-system consulting on the marketplace product, alongside the earlier-stated Laravel backend work (per the parent case study).
- **3 September 2019** — a small technical/hardware sizing question (server spec check: i5-4460, 8GB RAM, GTX/GT730 2GB) suggests Amir was also consulted on infra/hardware decisions.
- **21 September 2020** — a large, low-signal dump of recruiting-channel/group links Amir shared for job-ad distribution — logistics, not substantive.
- **4–6 October 2020** — the tail end of this stint: paperwork/settlement logistics. Amir flags a discrepancy about whether a "حسن انجام کار" (performance/completion) note needs to be written for a payment, then mails physical documents (ID/address proof) to Masoud, apologizing for forgetting a couple of items. This reads as an offboarding/final-settlement exchange rather than an active project handoff — the last substantive activity of stint 3.

## Coda — two isolated technical favors, no ongoing relationship (2022–2023)

The chat goes essentially silent from October 2020 onward, with only two more pings, each a single isolated exchange with no surrounding context or discussion:

- **14 October 2020 / 25 October 2022** — content-free forwards (a PDF document, a forwarded photo) — not substantive.
- **27 December 2023** — the final exchange in the entire chat history, a short access/login troubleshooting favor:

> **27.12.2023 16:08–16:17** — Masoud Abadi: اکانت هم maintainer هست
> Amir Yousefi: میگه invalid login
> Masoud Abadi: امیر سالار گفت مستقیم باهات لینک میشه
> Amir Yousefi: آره حل شد

This is the last message in the conversation. There is no explicit goodbye or handoff — the relationship simply ends here, on a small resolved technical favor, eight years and three separate working stints after it began.

# Sample sites shortlist (based on your positioning)

Date: May 27, 2026  
Goal: Inspiration for a personal consulting website focused on SaaS founders, premium advisory buyers, AI-era strategy services, and a "Book a strategy call" funnel.

## Your positioning constraints used for selection
- Independent consultant (not job-seeking profile)
- Consulting-first model (strategy/advisory before productized assets)
- Audience: SaaS founders + premium consulting buyers
- Broader positioning now, narrower later with traction
- English-first website
- Private pricing (for now)

---

## A) Strong direct-fit references (start here)

### 1) Mathew Dostal - Fractional CTO
URL: `https://mdostal.com/fractional-cto`

Why it fits:
- Clear consulting proposition for founders and executives.
- Strong split between audiences and engagement models.
- Excellent conversion flow with discovery-call CTA.

What to borrow:
- "Who I help" section with audience-specific pain points.
- Clear service menu (advisory, fractional, sprint).
- FAQ section that pre-qualifies prospects.

Watch out:
- This site publishes pricing publicly. You want private pricing, so keep structure but hide numbers.

---

### 2) Paul Okhrem - AI-fluent Fractional CTO
URL: `https://paul-okhrem.com/fractional-cto/`

Why it fits:
- Premium positioning in the AI era.
- Strong emphasis on "operator-grade" credibility and clear differentiation.
- Excellent for high-trust, high-ticket advisory framing.

What to borrow:
- Distinctive positioning statement ("AI-fluent" style differentiation).
- Scenario-based sections ("when to hire me").
- "What this role covers" clarity for executive buyers.

Watch out:
- Tone is highly premium and very niche; keep your version broader for now as planned.

---

### 3) Kathy Keating - CTO Advisory
URL: `https://kathkeating.com/cto-advisory/`

Why it fits:
- Advisory-first narrative (strategy, alignment, leadership).
- Strong language for executive audience (CEOs, CTOs, boards).
- Good process framing without technical clutter.

What to borrow:
- Advisory methodology section (Assess -> Plan -> Scale style).
- "Where I add value" blocks.
- Trust-building bio and testimonial placement.

Watch out:
- Very executive/governance heavy. Keep enough practical product/engineering signals for founder-stage buyers.

## Recommended use for your website v1

### Build your page architecture from this pattern
1. Hero: broad strategic advisory positioning (AI-era, SaaS founders).
2. Who I help: startup founders + premium consulting buyers.
3. Service menu: strategy session, advisory, audit, selective implementation.
4. Case highlights: TodaysCrypto, Sayman, Oxin.
5. Method/process: simple 3-step advisory framework.
6. FAQ: pricing/privacy, engagement shape, expected outcomes.
7. CTA: **Book a strategy call**.

### Keep these aligned with your decisions
- English only
- Private pricing
- Consulting-first
- Broad positioning now, narrower later by traction

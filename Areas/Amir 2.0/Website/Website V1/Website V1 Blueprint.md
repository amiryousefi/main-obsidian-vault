# Website V1 blueprint

Status: build-ready draft  
Date: May 27, 2026

## 1) V1 objective

Create a consulting-first personal site that:
- attracts SaaS founders and premium consulting buyers,
- positions Amir as an AI-era product and technology advisor,
- converts traffic into booked strategy calls.

Primary goal (phase 1): audience growth + qualified inbound conversations.  
Secondary goal (phase 2): consulting revenue and repeat advisory engagements.

---

## 2) Positioning foundation

I help SaaS and e-commerce teams make better product and technology decisions in the AI era, then execute with clarity and speed.

- Entry offer: AI-era strategy/advisory services
- Headline audience: SaaS founders + premium consulting service seekers

## 5) CTA funnel design (V1)

## Funnel flow
1. Visitor lands on Home
2. Reads positioning + proof + service fit
3. Clicks "Book a strategy call"
4. Completes qualification form
5. Schedules call
6. Receives pre-call confirmation + prep questions

## Qualification form (V1 fields)
- Name
- Work email
- Company name + website
- Company stage (idea, post-MVP, growth, scale)
- Primary challenge (open text)
- Desired outcome in next quarter
- Engagement preference:
  - strategy only
  - strategy + implementation
  - ongoing advisory
- Region/timezone
- Optional budget comfort range (can be hidden initially)

## Qualification logic
- High-fit leads:
  - SaaS/e-commerce
  - clear business problem
  - urgency and decision authority present
- Medium-fit:
  - problem is real but not yet scoped
- Low-fit:
  - looking for employment/hiring only
  - no clear challenge or commitment

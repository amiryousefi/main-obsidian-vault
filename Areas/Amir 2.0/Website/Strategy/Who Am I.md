# Who am I (draft)

## One-line version
I am Amir Yousefi, a full-stack web engineer and technology leader who builds scalable products and helps teams turn ideas into reliable software.

## Short bio
I am a full-stack developer with more than 15 years of experience across backend, frontend, architecture, and delivery leadership. I have worked as a CTO and lead developer in product and startup environments, and I enjoy both hands-on engineering and technical decision-making.

My work spans Laravel, React, Next.js, Python, and modern web product architecture. I have built and scaled systems in domains like media platforms, e-commerce, automation, and data-driven tools.

## Extended bio
I started in web development in the early 2010s and gradually moved into roles with more ownership over architecture, team leadership, and product direction. Over the years, I have worked on high-traffic and business-critical platforms, from news and e-commerce systems to online media products.

Today, I combine CTO-level strategic thinking with practical implementation. I care about writing maintainable systems, shipping useful features, and helping teams work with clarity and speed. I am especially motivated by projects where technology creates measurable value for people and organizations.

Alongside product work, I contribute to open source and share technical knowledge through articles and videos, particularly around Laravel, Python, automation, and developer workflows.

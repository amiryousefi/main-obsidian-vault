## 2) Strategic interpretation (what this means)

### Core positioning direction
Position as an **independent AI-era product and technology advisor** who can both:
- shape product/technology direction at leadership level, and
- execute critical technical work when needed.

This keeps you out of a narrow "framework developer" box and supports premium consulting positioning.

### Practical offer stack (recommended)
1. **Primary revenue (now): Consulting and advisory**
   - Product/tech strategy sessions
   - Architecture and delivery audits
   - Fractional CTO advisory
2. **Secondary revenue (next): Productized consulting assets**
   - E-books, playbooks, templates, checklists
3. **Selective implementation projects**
   - Limited-scope builds where strategy + execution are both needed

## 3) Pricing model options (recommended progression)

Because traction is still growing, avoid starting with only one model.

### Option A: Intro fixed-scope package + upsell retainer (recommended start)
- **Why it fits:** low-friction entry for new clients and clear outcomes.
- **How it works:**
  - Package 1: "Strategy or Architecture Audit" (fixed scope, fixed price)
  - Package 2: "Roadmap and Decision Support" monthly retainer
- **Benefit:** builds trust quickly, then converts to recurring work.

### Option B: Call-based consulting + project blocks
- **Why it fits:** if prospects are uncertain and need lighter commitment.
- **How it works:**
  - Paid advisory calls (single or bundle)
  - Project blocks for deeper execution after call outcomes
- **Benefit:** fastest path to first paid engagements and testimonials.

### Option C: Premium retainer-only
- **Why it fits:** best when authority and inbound demand are already strong.
- **Risk now:** may reduce conversion before market trust is established.

### Recommendation for your current stage
Start with **Option A + Option B**, then move toward stronger retainer positioning once proof and demand increase.

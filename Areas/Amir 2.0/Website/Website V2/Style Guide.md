# Style Guide (Website V2)

Date: May 27, 2026  
Mode: Futuristic premium  
Audience: SaaS founders and premium advisory buyers

## 1) Brand intent

This design language should communicate:
- strategic clarity
- advanced capability
- premium trust

The site should feel like a high-signal advisory interface, not a generic portfolio.

---

## 2) Design principles

1. **Narrative first, conversion second**  
   Build trust before pushing calls to action.
2. **Depth over noise**  
   Use layered surfaces and subtle glow, avoid visual clutter.
3. **Executive readability**  
   Content must be scan-friendly and structured.
4. **Restraint as quality**  
   Premium impression comes from control, not excess effects.

---

## 3) Color system

### Core
- `#060b16` - background
- `#0f1a33` - base surface
- `#152345` - elevated surface
- `#eaf1ff` - primary text
- `#9eb0d9` - secondary text

### Accent
- `#6ea8ff` - electric blue
- `#8f7dff` - violet
- `#49d6ff` - cyan

Usage rule:
- Keep accent usage controlled (about 20% of visible UI).
- Prefer gradients only for key emphasis and primary CTA.

---

## 4) Typography

### Headings
- `Space Grotesk` (fallback: `Inter`, sans-serif)
- Bold, compact, strategic tone

### Body
- `Inter` (fallback: system sans)
- Comfortable line-height for long-form narrative text

Tone:
- Headlines = strategic and atmospheric
- Body = practical and precise

---

## 5) Layout and spacing

- Max container: `1180px`
- Text-heavy blocks: `760-820px`
- Section spacing: `96-132px` on desktop
- Card radius: `16-22px`
- Visual hierarchy must prioritize narrative progression

---

## 6) Graphics direction

Preferred graphic motifs:
- orbital rings
- grid horizons
- data streams
- glow nodes

Preferred format:
- SVG-based custom graphics

Avoid:
- generic stock photos
- noisy 3D gimmicks
- decorative elements without narrative role

---

## 7) Components

### CTA button
- Gradient fill (`#6ea8ff -> #8f7dff`)
- Soft glow shadow
- Clear, concise text ("Book a strategy call")

### Card
- Dark translucent panel
- 1px cool border
- Subtle top accent line

### Pill/tag
- Rounded badge with low-contrast surface
- Used for capability signals (Strategy, Architecture, AI, Advisory)

---

## 8) Motion rules

Use motion to guide attention, never to distract.

Recommended:
- soft hover lift on cards
- subtle CTA glow intensification
- optional section reveal on scroll

Constraints:
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- keep transitions fast and understated

---

## 9) Content + visual pairing

- Every section must answer a strategic question.
- Pair atmospheric lines with concrete outcomes.
- Keep one core message per section.
- Use whitespace to indicate confidence.

---

## 10) CTA and conversion style

Primary CTA:
- **Book a strategy call**

CTA language pattern:
- invitation over urgency
- confidence over hype

Example:
"If you are navigating a high-stakes product or technology decision, let's map the next move."

---

## 11) Personal-first adjustments (May 28, 2026)

- Keep homepage sequence as: **person -> principles -> proof -> collaboration -> CTA**.
- Use a **battle-tested builder** tone (calm, decisive, experienced).
- Keep story professional and humanized (specific signals, low personal drama).
- Include subtle exclusivity messaging for premium positioning.
- Add personal visuals (portrait/editorial treatment) blended with futuristic graphics.
- Keep geography references minimal unless directly relevant to proof.

---

## 12) Refinement update (May 28, 2026 · late)

- Keep design **minimal + futuristic hybrid**:
  - minimal by default
  - graphics where they strengthen meaning.
- Add emotional depth ("soul") without losing professionalism.
- Use exclusivity language carefully:
  - preferred: **Selective advisory engagements each quarter**
  - written as honest context, not marketing pressure.
- Ensure homepage simultaneously serves:
  1) personal introduction
  2) informative/inspiring thought leadership
  3) work promotion (products/services)
- Case proof order on homepage:
  - Oxin -> TodaysCrypto -> Sayman

---

## 13) CTA posture recommendation

- Keep primary CTA as **Book a strategy call**.
- Balance it with low-pressure supporting copy to stay credible at current stage.
- Suggested supporting line:
  - "Start with a focused fit conversation."

# Personal fingerprint intake (what to provide next)

Date: May 28, 2026  
Goal: identify the exact inputs needed to make the website deeply personal, credible, and non-generic.

## 1) Portrait and visual identity inputs

Please provide:
- Primary portrait photo (high resolution)
- 1 alternate portrait (optional)
- Preference:
  - dark editorial or neutral clean background
- Permission:
  - can portrait be color-graded for website style (yes/no)

Why it matters:
- this is the strongest personal authority signal in the hero and About sections.

---

## 2) Signature personal statements (short)

Provide 3-5 short lines you truly believe in.
Examples of useful format:
- "I optimize for decisions that still make sense 12 months later."
- "I care about the system, not only the sprint."

Why it matters:
- these lines become "soul anchors" across homepage and How I Think page.

---

## 3) Professional-humanized story moments

Provide 2-3 brief turning points (2-4 sentences each):
- challenge/context
- what changed in how you think
- resulting principle

Keep tone professional (not private life details).

Why it matters:
- this creates authenticity without becoming informal.

---

## 4) Field principles content

For each principle, provide:
- principle name (short)
- one real-world example
- one decision heuristic ("when X happens, do Y")

Target: 6-10 principles.

Why it matters:
- this powers your informative/inspiring layer.

---

## 5) Case-study enrichment inputs

For each case (Oxin, TodaysCrypto, Sayman), provide:
- starting challenge
- your role and scope
- 2-3 key decisions
- 1-3 outcomes (numbers preferred)
- one "what this taught me" insight

Why it matters:
- improves proof quality and lead conversion.

---

## 6) Product and service promotion inputs

Products:
- names, short descriptions, status (live/coming soon)
- intended audience
- expected format (ebook/template/playbook/video)

Services:
- final offer names
- ideal client profile per offer
- expected engagement shape

Why it matters:
- ensures promotion feels intentional, not bolted on.

---

## 7) Trust signals to provide

- testimonials or short endorsements (even 1-2 lines)
- logos/brands (if allowed)
- public talks, articles, interviews, or notable mentions

Why it matters:
- premium audiences look for third-party validation.

---

## 8) Recommended minimum pack before V3 visual polish

1. One final portrait
2. Three signature statements
3. Two story turning points
4. Basic detail blocks for the 3 cases
5. Final wording for exclusivity line

Once this pack is provided, the website can be made distinctly personal and hard to imitate.

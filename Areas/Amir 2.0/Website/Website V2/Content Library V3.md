## 1) Field Principles (converted to your tone)

These are rewritten from your core values in a battle-tested builder voice.

### 1) Write to think, not to decorate
I document to sharpen decisions, align people, and make progress measurable.  
If it is not clear in writing, it is not clear in execution.

### 2) Build all the way to done
I value outputs, not activity.  
Every day should end with something real: shipped capability, improved system, clearer structure, or better direction.

### 3) Prioritize what actually moves the system
Not every task deserves equal energy.  
I start with the highest-leverage work and protect focus around meaningful outcomes.

### 4) Stay present in the details
Quality is not accidental.  
I stay engaged from definition to delivery so important details are not lost between intention and execution.

### 5) Leave things better than I found them
Working is the baseline. Distinctive is the target.  
I optimize for outcomes that are stronger, cleaner, and more valuable than what existed before.

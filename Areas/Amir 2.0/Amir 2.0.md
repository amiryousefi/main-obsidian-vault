# Amir 2.0

Moving from Amir 1.0 — the ambitious operator stuck in positioning paralysis —
to a Builder-CEO and Creative Technologist. Not a rebrand: claiming an identity
already earned, and making it visible.

**The central finding:** 15+ years of real technical experience, three CTO roles,
a gaming company and a premium studio in planning — behind a public presence
frozen in 2019. The gap between private capability and public reality is the
single biggest obstacle.

**The motto:** کار معنادار — *meaningful work*. An operational filter, not an
aspiration: if an action doesn't create lasting value, solve a real problem, or
build leverage, it isn't meaningful work.
